# wiki/raw — original sources, never edited

Every source your AI ingests lands here first, unchanged: the PDF, the article, the exported
chat, the photo of a whiteboard. Named `YYYY-MM-DD-slug.ext`.

Two reasons this folder exists:

1. **Provenance.** A page in `wiki/pages/` can say "this came from here" and point at a real
   file, so a claim can always be traced back.
2. **Re-reading.** Understanding improves. A source you distilled badly in January can be read
   again in June without hunting for the original.

Nothing in here is ever edited or deleted. The distilled version lives in `wiki/pages/`.
