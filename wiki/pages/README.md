# wiki/pages — the knowledge itself

One topic per file. Short lowercase-dash filenames: `supplier-pricing.md`, `competitor-moves.md`,
`how-we-quote.md`.

Each page carries `tags:` in frontmatter, `[[wikilinks]]` to related pages, a `## Related`
section at the bottom, and — for anything claimed from outside — its source.

Empty on purpose. Your AI fills it as you feed it. See `../SCHEMA.md` for the page template.
